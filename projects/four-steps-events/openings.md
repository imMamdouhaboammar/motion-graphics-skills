# Opening directions (0 to 3.4 s)

All three use the same VO, the same Thmanyah faces and the same campaign art direction. Each is switchable in `index.html?opening=A|B|C`. Previews: `renders/first-opening-A.mp4`, `-B.mp4`, `-C.mp4`. Side-by-side still at 0.9 s: `assets/ref/openings-compare-0.9s.png`.

## A. Typography-led editorial collage

Frame 0 already holds a 440 px «آخر» cropped by the right edge, a stage lamp throwing a flat paper-coloured beam behind the type, and the edge of the frame in shadow. «فعالية» and «كبيرة» land on their VO words (0.36, 0.82) from opposite sides, each with a short motion smear. A truss slams through between the lines, a broadcast camera slides in, and the words compress upward to make room. On «منبهر» (2.84) a ticket stub snaps in on a blue starburst.

## B. Object-led stage transformation

A printed floor plan in perspective; the stage block stands up into an elevation, the truss drops on, lamps appear, two blue cones fire on «منبهر». Headline set at 150 px across the top.

## C. Depth-led paper world

Three planes (stage and screen far, seat rows and camera mid, pass and cable near) with a slow push through them. The frame drops to navy at 1.1 s and the subline appears in paper colour.

## Scores (1 to 5)

| Criterion | A | B | C |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scroll-stopping strength in the first second | **5** Giant type, lamp, beam and truss in the first 10 frames | 2 First frame is mostly empty paper and a small plan | 3 Busy, but everything sits small and centred |

| Originality | 4 | 3 | 4 |

| Hand-off into the rest of the film | **5** Leaves a quiet paper frame for the question, then the plan card rises for beat 2 | 2 Uses up the plan-to-stage idea that beat 2 needs | 3 |
| **Total** | **28** | 18 | 19 |

## Choice: A

A wins on the two things that matter most in a feed, the first second and readability, and it is the only one that leaves beat 2's plan-to-stage transformation fresh. It is also the least "busy by numbers": the frame holds three big words and three objects, and the density comes from scale, not count.

Changes made to A after the comparison:
- The lanyard pass was removed from the opening because it covered the compressed «فعالية». The ticket stub already carries the "event pass" idea, and the pass returns properly in beat 5.
- The subline «حضرتها وطلعت منها» now exits before the blue burst lands, so no ink text ever sits on blue.
- The question lifts out of frame as the plan card rises, instead of being covered by it.
