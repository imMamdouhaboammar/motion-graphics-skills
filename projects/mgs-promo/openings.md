# Opening studies

Three 2.6 s studies over the same VO opening («شايف الموشن هذا؟», 0.08 to 1.21 s) and the same flip to paper at 1.50 s. Source: `studies/open-{a,b,c}/index.html`. Sheet: `studies/sheets/openings-abc.png` (t = 0, 0.2, 0.5, 1.0, 1.4, 1.9 s).

| Study | Mechanism | First frame | Phone read | Originality | Handoff to B2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A, object-led | A paper film strip races in and locks with «الموشن» in the centre frame | weak: a blurred sliver on black | word too small inside the frame | film strip is a stock motion cliché | neutral |
| B, typography-led | «الموشن» enters as five horizontal slices out of registration and snaps into alignment; a running timecode counts frames | strong: huge broken letterforms already moving | reads by 0.3 s | fits a print/registration material and the reference's type-first grammar | clean hard flip to paper |
| C, terminal-led | A clay block cursor blinks, then paints the word right to left | weak: a lone orange bar | reads by 0.6 s | good tie to the cursor motif | good |

**Chosen: B.** The first frame promises motion before a word is heard, and the type is the hero at phone size. The clay cursor from C is kept as the film's recurring agent from B3 onward instead of the opening.

Fixes carried into the film:
- Study B cropped the alef of «الموشن» at the frame edge, so the word read as «لموشن». The film keeps the whole word inside the frame and crops nothing that is read.
- The paper texture was too strong and showed its tile. Opacity drops to about 7 % with a larger scale.
