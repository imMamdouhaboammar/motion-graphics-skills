## Recording notes

- Pronounce "SFX-picker" as "S-F-X picker"; read "reel-export" as "reel export".
- Leave a short breath before line 3, and let "Every frame is code" land slowly.
- Exact timing doesn't matter. I cut the visuals to your recording, so read naturally.
- Send WAV or high-quality MP3, with a second of silence at the start and end, recorded in a quiet room, no music.
