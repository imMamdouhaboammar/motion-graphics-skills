# Mix brief: Motion Graphics Skills promo v2

## Project brief

A 30 s vertical English promo (1080 × 1920) for the motion-graphics-skills repo, with a recorded voice over.
Feedback on v1: the hip-hop bed was tiring across the whole film, the motion felt traditional, and the owner asked for a fresher idea built from more than one reference.

## Three hypotheses

### 1. Coherent route: "The Curve" (selected)
**Concept:** a single coral easing curve, drawn live like in a graph editor, directs the film.
- **Mechanism:** the curve draws on, bends, and carries the camera. Its keyframe diamonds pop open into each skill. A playhead sweeps across, and each crossing flips the world between paper and void.
- **Inheritance:** W1 curve and diamonds, B paper and void rhythm plus red key word, A letter settle plus card on a wire, mascots from the repo.
- **What stays stable:** one curve, one playhead, two worlds, one key word per line.
- **What it refuses:** starbursts, countdown numbers, emoji, stock props doing jokes, and a beat that runs the whole film.

### 2. Tension route: "Neon Notebook"
Paper sketches (A's grid) get "rendered" by a neon void wipe (B). Each skill is a pencil sketch that the void turns into a lit, finished frame.
- **Strength:** a strong before and after.
- **Why not chosen:** it needs hand-drawn assets for every beat, and it reads as a render effect more than as a system.

### 3. Constraint route: "One Card"
The whole film happens inside one prompt card (A), which grows to full frame. B's beats play as the card's output, one phrase at a time.
- **Strength:** very clear.
- **Why not chosen:** it's close to v1's card grammar, and the motion stays flat.

## Creative distance audit (selected route)

- Three sources shape it, none dominates (helper source share 0.33 each; no warnings).
- Scene order follows the voice-over script, which matches neither reference.
- No protected object survives: no starbursts, emoji, bulbs, briefcases or countdown digits.
- It can be described without naming a reference: "one easing curve directs a reel about code-driven motion."
- It stays specific to this repo: easing curves and keyframes are the material of motion design, and the mascots belong to the repo.

## Rejected derivative patterns

- A's numbered countdown with starbursts at each number.
- B's suited figure with a speech-bubble head, and its emoji props.
- B's exact grey road shape and pinned-card checklist.

## Sound

The voice over is the master timeline. The music is a quiet pad that sits under the voice, with no drum loop running the whole film; sound effects follow the curve (draw, snap, pop, playhead whoosh).
