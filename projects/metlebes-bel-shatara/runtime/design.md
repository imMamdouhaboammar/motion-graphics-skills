# Art direction
Flat pastel fills, dark plum 5px imperfect constructed outlines. Teal angular-haired lead, rounded lavender-shirt client. The colored characters, embodied contradiction and connected object transformations are the dominant system. Cream warm rooms become dark blue for struggle, then return to cream. Primary anchor is the acting character or its directly handled object. No display text or external visual assets. Shapes and path transforms carry transitions, with reduced visual density in the nighttime close-and-reopen scene. Safe area 120px except deliberate foreground fills and traveling paths.
