# Mix brief: قاموس المكتب, episode 1

## Project brief

A 41.5 s vertical Arabic reel about psychological safety in meetings, cut to the owner's voice over. Voice and screen play ping pong.

## References

| ID | Reference | Role |
|---|---|---|
| A | handwritten maths formulas drifting on a navy chalkboard, depth of field | primary |
| B | red monochrome head with a glass dome showing a brain, a "?" and a "!", an eye above, endless spiral zoom | primary |
| C | pale lavender motivational reel: simple blue characters, one accent colour, calendar card, bars, a dark spotlight row | primary |

## Reference genome

| Source | Gene | Principle | Protected | Transform |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | material | a field of half-legible writing at several depths, blurred by distance | the maths content | becomes office jargon (KPI, ASAP, «تسليم الخميس») drifting behind the meeting |
| B | object | the head is a window: what the person thinks is visible in a glass dome | the exact figure, the brain-cloud art | every team member carries a dome holding the question they will not ask |
| B | transition | zooming into the head opens a new world | the spiral loop timing | the zoom enters one member's head to show why they stay quiet |
| B | object | an eye watches from above | its placement | it becomes the boss: the eye opens on «المدير» and glances at a clock |
| C | character | soft faceless figures whose eyes carry the emotion | the blue mascot design | the team: slate on the chalkboard, blue once the room is safe |
| C | colour | one accent colour on a pale field; a dark row with one spotlit figure | the palette values | lavender and blue mark the safe world; the spotlight carries «ولو خايف تبعته» |
| C | object | a tilted card lands to define a word; bars show a result | the calendar | a dictionary card defines «أمان نفسي»; five bars show Google's five dynamics |

## Hypotheses

1. **Three worlds (selected).** Chalkboard meeting → red inside-the-head → lavender safe room, then back to the board for the payoff. The colour of the world tells you how safe it is.
2. **One head.** The whole film inside a single dome. Strong, but the team and the chat beat get lost.
3. **Dictionary only.** Kinetic type on the card. Clear, but it drops the characters that make the subject easy to watch.

## Creative distance

All three references shape the film; none supplies its layout or scene order (the voice over sets the order).
No protected object survives: the maths, the red figure and the blue mascot are all replaced by objects built for this brief.
