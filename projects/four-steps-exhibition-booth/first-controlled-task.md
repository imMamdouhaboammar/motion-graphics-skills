# First Controlled Task — Kling Motion Proof (Proposed)

**Purpose:** Prove the visitor action and booth geography that are difficult to create convincingly with code. This is a shot brief for later pricing, not a submitted generation.

## Candidate beat

“بس فيه جناح يخلّيك توقف، تدخل، وتسأل عن اللي يقدمه”

Potential reference pair from Drive: `01_exhibition_booth_cluster.png` for the wider exhibition context and `05_visitor_stopping.png` for the visitor-action reference. Inspect both source images and confirm their rights before using them as model references.

## Direction to preserve for a future prompt

- Photoreal, documentary-style trade-show footage with natural human pacing and physically grounded contact.
- One clear action: visitor walks through a real exhibition aisle, notices one booth, eases to a stop, turns, and takes a few steps toward its open entrance.
- Maintain stable booth geometry, lighting, neighboring exhibits, and visitor appearance throughout the shot.
- Use a restrained, motivated camera move; no teleporting, morphing, impossible body motion, beauty-ad styling, or exaggerated reactions.
- Keep signs, logos, screens, and readable text blank or out of focus in generated footage; add verified branding and Arabic typography in post.
- Use the supplied visual references only to define the actual booth and visitor continuity after inspection; do not invent project/year/location claims.

## Before submitting to Kling

1. Locate and inspect the actual WAV and candidate image references.
2. Check current Kling model, image-reference roles, supported duration/aspect ratio, and live cost for this exact shot.
3. Record quote and proposed settings in the credit ledger.
4. Obtain approval for that one quoted job and its credit ceiling.
5. Submit one proof, inspect the rendered frames and playback, and accept/revise/reject before any further generation.
