# Reference study

Client-supplied reference: a 54.6 s, 720×1280, 30 fps Saudi lead-generation case-study film. The file itself is not in this repository (client asset).

Method: contact sheets at 2 fps across the whole film, `select='gt(scene,0.25)'` for cut times, five full-size frames, and pixel samples for colour.

## Grammar

| Element | Observed | Used in this film |
|---|---|---|
| Ground | Cream paper `#ECE4D2` with visible fibre and grain | Same, with `paper-fibre.png` and three cycling grain frames |
| Brand colour | Deep teal `#2F675D`, darker `#1E4A42` for shadow and depth | Same pair plus `#163A33` and `#4F8A7D` for depth |
| Masthead | Three small labels across the top of every frame | «جدار / إدارة الأزمات / تقييم الجاهزية», green on paper, cream on green |
| Type | Heavy Arabic sans, word-by-word rise, many words inside green or cream bars | Thmanyah Sans 700 to 900; words rise in masks; bars wipe right to left |
| Numerals | One huge Arabic-Indic numeral with a stepped extrusion, lower third a green panel | «٣» at 1000 px in Thmanyah Serif Display, extruded, green panel below |
| Layout | Split frames: paper above, green panel below | Used for «مجاني؟», «٣ أشياء», the browser scene |
| Objects | Pinned paper note on a green grid; seesaw with pawns; browser windows over a blurred stack | Pinned note for «تقييم جاهزية مجاني»; seesaw for «للطرفين»; browser for «إذا اسمك» |
| Imagery | AI-generated photographic collage (people, props) | Replaced by flat vector illustration. Declared difference, not an oversight |
| Cuts | Mostly hard cuts, about every 1 to 3 s, on the VO phrase | Hard cuts on phrase boundaries; two carried transitions (flood, door) |

## What makes it memorable, and what we added

The reference repeats one move: a phrase lands as bold type, one object illustrates it, cut. This film keeps that rhythm and adds one recurring device the brand name offers: the wall (جدار). Water rises and swallows the frame at «السيل», a wall is built brick by brick at «جدار», and a door opens in it at «تفتح الباب قبل الأزمة», the camera travelling through the door into the next scene.
