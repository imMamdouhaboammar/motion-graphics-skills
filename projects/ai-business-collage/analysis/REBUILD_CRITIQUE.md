# V1 strict rebuild critique

V1 fails the requested reference fidelity. Export validity and the presence of an animation mechanism do not establish visual fidelity. Earlier PASS labels for "overhead", "card field", and "pressure" meant that those broad mechanisms existed; they did not test occupied scene composition, overlap, camera displacement, photographed context, or asset relevance. Those labels must not be reused as creative acceptance.

## Evidence and reference choice

Primary reference: `input/reference.mp4`, 720×1280, 30 fps, 38.704807 s. Its newly attached `(2)` copy is the same primary reference. Exact cut-frame evidence is in `reference_measurements/sentinel_1.jpg` through `sentinel_3.jpg`; large critical shot evidence is in `reference_measurements/critical_source_geometry.jpg` and `ref052.png`.

The 47.690930 s AQOUL attachment is a handheld recording of an editing monitor playing a green/turquoise live-action commercial. Software timeline and monitor surroundings persist throughout. It is not a second collage reference. It supports a cinematic quality expectation, but copying its footage, green palette, or monitor-recording wrapper would be an incorrect change of reference. Dense evidence: `reference_measurements/alternate_contact.jpg`.

## Rebuild standard

Match the source shot's occupied world and framing, not its palette alone. Every new shot needs a mapped source shot, sampled start/middle/end frames, subject fill and overlap measurements, camera displacement/orientation, and an asset chosen for the narrative verb. Source photographic context matters: room, floor, window shadows, workstation objects and people must form one coherent environment. Scattered PNGs on cream do not substitute for that world. Calm blank space is concentrated in assembly, map setup and closing; it is not a global template.

## Motion and texture diagnosis

1. Source has occupied photographic worlds alternating with sparse ivory punctuation. V1 uses ivory as default stage and places stock cutouts on top, reversing density hierarchy.
2. Source photographed floor/light/objects create depth before animation. A CSS shadow around isolated PNGs cannot replace coherent lighting and perspective.
3. Source object interactions carry motion. V1 substitutes generic28-frame pop-in and uniform parent scale/translate across unrelated pictures.
4. Source camera movement changes what is revealed: desk teams, map destinations, more finished creatives. V1 moves texture or blank card margins, so displacement produces no discovery.
5. Palette and fonts are necessary but do not establish style. Stock coffee, bottles and empty boxes weaken semantic relevance of new narration.
6. Rotating the card field is not sufficient; source card shape, crop, face occupation, variety, subject scale and shadows must be matched together.
7. Default persistent top headers create presentation-slide composition. Source text inhabits the action area and selectively overlaps photos/props.
8. Grain must be subtle material detail, not global overlay. Paper props can have paper texture, while photographed rooms retain crisp natural texture.

## Required review gates for rebuilt scenes

- Strict PASS only after source/target start-mid-end comparison demonstrates subject fill, world occupancy, camera direction and reveal, contextual density, object overlap and asset relevance.
- Each shot report names its source frame/timestamp and records measured target bboxes, not just "camera present".
- Priority gates: fullroom overhead worlds; intrusive front-pressure; dense meaningful diagonal field. Failing any of these blocks claiming reference fidelity.
- Fixes require before/after target evidence. Safe encoding and connected Arabic do not waive visual gate failures.
- Audio remains immutable; stage beats around its real keyword times, without extending narration or globally retiming it.
