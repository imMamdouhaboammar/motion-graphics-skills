## NON-NEGOTIABLE TIMING METHOD

Do not allocate shot boundaries by equal-duration slides, and do not blindly stretch scenes by 48.35 / 38.70 = **1.2493**. That ratio is an initial reference-derived scaffold only.

1. Decode WAV; create speech activity/phrase landmarks. Prefer actual forced alignment or word-time analysis with human waveform review and available approved tooling, not invented timecodes.
2. Create `analysis/voice_alignment.json` with start/end/confidence and text for every spoken clause, and mark manual approximations explicitly.
3. Detect source scene cuts and camera moves with ffmpeg/ffprobe, scene thumbnails, time-domain sampling and direct playback. Save `analysis/reference_report.json`, `analysis/reference_contact.jpg`, `analysis/camera_motion_table.csv`, `analysis/reference_motion_dna.md`. Distinguish actual cuts from optical motion/occlusion.
4. Use the storyboard windows below only as **guide windows** (phase order and planned camera mechanism). Snap transitions to actual voice clauses/pauses; keep source's fast / slow alternation and weighted duration proportions. Most target scenes may need nested micro-cuts.
5. Start important visual actions at the audible keyword, not arbitrarily at a shot boundary. A label may enter a few frames before the corresponding narration but must remain semantically accurate.
6. At 30 fps, design in integer frames. Normalize sampleable states so frame N can be rendered deterministically without dependence on previous playback frames.
7. When narration contains a genuine hold/silence, let a cutout land or a type beat breathe. Never populate every gap with generic movement.
