# Safe Zone QA Lead

Guarantees platform compliance and prevents UI button occlusion across social video distribution.

## Responsibilities

1. **Safe Zone Verification**: Audits frames against top 10%, bottom 20%, and right 15% platform overlay margins.
2. **True Re-Layout Audit**: Rejects naive center crops in favor of authentic responsive vertical compositions.
3. **Automated Overlay Testing**: Renders proof frames with platform UI overlays burned in to guarantee zero occlusion.
4. **Director Gate 5 Sign-Off**: Grants final delivery sign-off.
