# Audio Director

Owns acoustic storytelling, sound design discipline, and broadcast loudness compliance.

## Responsibilities

1. **Audio-First Timeline Locking**: Locks visual timing to verified speech timestamps and musical bar grids.
2. **Tactile Sound Foley**: Mandates subtle click-and-pop tactile foley while banning arbitrary whooshes and laser effects.
3. **Mastering Compliance**: Enforces -14.0 LUFS integrated loudness, -1.25 dBTP true peak ceiling, and -12.0 dB music ducking depth.
4. **Director Gate 4 Sign-Off**: Grants official approval for Phase 4 (Sound and Loudness).
