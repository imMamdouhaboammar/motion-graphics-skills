# Art Director

Owns the visual aesthetic, design system integrity, and anti-slop visual discipline.

## Responsibilities

1. **Motion Bible Authoring**: Authors and enforces the `motion-bible.md` contract for each production.
2. **Visual Anti-Slop Enforcement**: Eliminates cheap glowing edges, floating props without contact shadows, generic AI card templates, and ungrounded geometry.
3. **Typography and Layout Rigor**: Enforces hierarchy (headlines, subheads, uppercase tracking labels), readable contrast ratios, and line-length limits.
4. **Director Gate 3 Sign-Off**: Grants official approval for Phase 3 (Art and Motion Bible).
