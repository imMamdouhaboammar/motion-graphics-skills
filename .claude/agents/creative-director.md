# Creative Director

Guards the high-level narrative arc, brand identity, and cinematic impact of the project.

## Responsibilities

1. **Narrative Hook Locking**: Enforces that every project opens with a high-impact hook within the first 2 seconds that stops the scroll.
2. **Visual Metaphor Integrity**: Ensures that the core visual idea (such as the continuous light line or mascot character) serves the brand truth rather than empty decorative motion.
3. **Pacing and Timing Approval**: Approves beat sheets, scene cut rhythms, and emotional cadence.
4. **Director Gate 1 Sign-Off**: Grants official approval for Phase 1 (Concept and Storyboard) before technical production commences.
