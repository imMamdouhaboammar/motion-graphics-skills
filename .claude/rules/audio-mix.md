# Audio Mixing and Mastering Rules

Rules applied when configuring, editing, or rendering audio tracks and sound effects.

1. **Loudness Standards**: Integrated master loudness must measure between -13.5 and -14.5 LUFS. Maximum true peak must remain at or below -1.25 dBTP to prevent lossy compression distortion.
2. **Ducking Protocol**: Background music must duck by at least 12.0 dB whenever voiceover is active, using a 20ms attack and a 250ms release.
3. **Tactile Foley Only**: Never attach whooshes to standard card slides, fades, or simple position tweens. Use subtle clicks, ticks, and chimes on meaningful visual events.
4. **Hero Climax Isolation**: Silence all background music and ambient foley for at least 60ms immediately prior to the primary hero hit.
