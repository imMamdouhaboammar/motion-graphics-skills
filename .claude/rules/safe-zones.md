# Safe Zone and Mobile Platform Rules

Rules applied when designing vertical 9:16 video compositions (1080 x 1920).

1. **Top Safe Margin**: Keep the top 170 pixels (10% of frame height) completely clear of essential headlines, faces, and brand marks to avoid camera cutouts and platform search bars.
2. **Bottom Safe Margin**: Keep the bottom 384 pixels (20% of frame height) completely clear of subtitles, call-to-action buttons, and fine print to avoid platform captions, usernames, and audio pills.
3. **Right Action Column**: Keep the right 162 pixels (15% of frame width) between Y=900px and Y=1500px clear of interactive graphics to avoid like, comment, and share icons.
4. **Overlay Proof**: Before finalizing any vertical render, generate a test frame with the platform overlay burned in to prove zero text collision.
