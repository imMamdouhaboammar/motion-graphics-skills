# Scene Handovers Without Cuts

Techniques for transitioning between visual spaces in an unbroken shot.

## 1. The Screen Fly-Through

The camera pushes directly into an illuminated interface element (such as an input field or confirmation dialog) until the element fills the entire viewport, transitioning into the next environment as a focal dive.

## 2. The Orthographic Pivot

Transition from a two-dimensional user interface to a three-dimensional physical landscape by rotating the camera axis 45 degrees while simultaneously scaling viewport dimensions.

## 3. The Particle Reveal

A glowing particle or line wraps around a three-dimensional object, depositing surface texture and transitioning from an active energy beam into passive physical material such as printed box ink.
