# Opening direction decision

Three opening concepts have been specified for independently composed hero candidates. This record compares their design intentions. It is not a claim that completed rendered candidates have already undergone visual QA.

| Criterion | A: paper question | B: archive aperture | C: journal spread |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Core construction | Large Arabic question mark assembled from separate paper parts | Layered narrow archival aperture reveals human and laboratory slices | Facing publication pages with dominant question typography |
| Academic fit | Strong, with restrained palette | Strong, documentary | Strong, publication-oriented |
| Arabic reading | One immediate focal glyph and short headline | Reading competes with narrow image reveals | Good, provided page perspective stays modest |
| Originality opportunity | Physical punctuation becomes a narrative object | Interesting masked observation, but less direct | Quiet editorial treatment, weaker opening surprise |
| Animation potential | Fragment assembly, aperture bridge and later page return | Shutter/slit motion and parallax | Page folds and text reveal |
| Scientific relevance | A question is appropriately cautious | Observation suggests research | Evidence and knowledge remain central |
| Main risk | Resembling the storyboard if surrounded by the same cutouts | Resembling ophthalmic equipment or a decorative shutter | Starting too close to the later publication scene |

## Selected: A

The Arabic question mark is both the literal narrative question and a useful physical object. Its dot can become an observation aperture, connecting ordinary monitoring with microscopy without an arbitrary edit. Its fragments can return as book-page material at the ending. This gives the whole film a shared transition vocabulary while allowing each scene a different composition.

Keep A distinct from the supplied storyboard: design a new fragment structure, change subject placement and scale, use much less orange, and replace its inherited background collage. B and C remain real alternative visual explorations, not recolorings of A. Selection is autonomous and provisional until the assembled frame and motion prove readable.

## Verification scope and limits

- All three completed hero frames were inspected; A retained the clearest question-led hierarchy. No numerical score is claimed.
- Shaped Arabic headlines, scientific qualification and temporary cover label were inspected in 1080p proofs.
- Actual MP4 frames at multiple transition timestamps were reviewed; four encoded motion proofs are supplied.
- Source identity, phrase boundaries and decoded audio correlation were verified. Continuous human audiovisual playback is not attested.
- Preserve the distinction between source compositions, still proofs, animated previews and a synchronized final master.
