# Creative direction: the journey of a question

Promote the forthcoming Arabic book «السكري.. هل اقتربت نهاية المرض؟» through curiosity, human connection and evidence. The recurring Arabic question mark links daily life, research, evaluation and the publication. The audience moves through the pages of scientific discovery without receiving a promise of a universal cure.

## Material and motion

A single light source, above-left, governs all paper shadows. Foreground fragments, photographic subjects, annotation and backgrounds remain independent. Parallax is applied only to physically separated layers. Scene 04 uses paper unfolding and linked lines instead of five interchangeable digital cards. Scene 05 deliberately reduces movement and density.

No wood, neon, ornamental formulas, repeated collage backgrounds, decorative tape or default fade transitions. Generated imagery supplies isolated visual components; essential Arabic copy is typeset independently. Biomedical graphics are explanatory drawings, never claimed as experimental micrographs.

## Scientific boundaries

Read `research/scientific-review.md` before composing captions. Distinguish beta cells from the whole islet. Stem-cell-derived islet work is one research direction, particularly relevant to type 1 diabetes, and does not establish a universal cure. The evidence progression is explanatory; safety evaluation overlaps clinical work and follow-up can continue after authorization. Avoid fabricated statistics, trial dates, journal documents and approval seals.
