## Overall judgement

The film is an actual layered motion production with coherent ivory, navy, teal and orange treatment, clean photographic cutouts, readable Arabic and a clear book endpoint. It is more than a flattened poster with a zoom. It avoids cure promises in its sampled text and labels the microscopy graphic as explanatory

The strict creative brief is not fully met by this first render. The blank first frame, concealed scientific qualification and entry-phase copy collisions require correction. The evidence section is operationally clear but its five similar cards limit originality. The common top-right heading, top-left scene counter and large clean text area make the sequence feel closer to a designed editorial presentation than the highest requested collage-film standard

Photographic identity and usage rights were not independently audited here. Generated people must remain explicitly fictional and must not imply an actual patient, institution or researcher endorsement. Simplified biomedical artwork is not a substitute for clinical evidence
