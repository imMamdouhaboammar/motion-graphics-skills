## Shared visual grammar

The references combine documentary cutouts with a small number of assertive geometric forms. Photographic scale anchors the composition; diagrams act as a secondary reading level. Pale paper margins are active negative space, not unused space. Controlled overlap makes the eye travel between objects. A small warm accent directs attention while monochrome photography and dark type retain authority.

The production identity uses independent photographic cutouts, original biomedical SVG diagrams, restrained paper structures and precisely shaped Arabic type. Orange is reserved for the question and relationships. Illustrations are labeled as illustrations. No reference image is to be inserted as a completed scene or animated by applying a camera zoom.

## Motion implications

1. Assemble the opening question mark from separate paper segments.
2. Use its dot as an observation aperture into ordinary life.
3. Reframe the monitoring aperture as a microscope field, with an explicit scale change into a cell diagram.
4. Extend diagram annotation lines into a continuous evidence pathway.
5. Unfold the evidence material into an editorial spread, then gather its sheets into a clearly temporary book cover.

These are designed transition intentions. Rendered temporal quality remains subject to review.
