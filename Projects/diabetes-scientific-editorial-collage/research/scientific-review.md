# Scientific review: diabetes editorial film

Reviewed 4 October 2026. Scope: narration and planned illustrations, not medical advice or an assertion that a particular investigational product is currently approved. The film can communicate research promise responsibly with the guardrails below.

## Core distinctions

- Type 1 diabetes involves immune destruction of insulin-producing pancreatic beta cells. Type 2 diabetes involves insulin resistance and insufficient insulin production relative to need. Do not depict all diabetes as one uniform beta-cell destruction mechanism.
- Pancreatic islets contain multiple cell types; beta cells produce insulin. A simplified cluster may highlight beta cells but should not label every cell in an islet as an insulin-producing beta cell.
- Stem-cell-derived islet research and deceased-donor islet transplantation are different approaches. FDA-approved Lantidra uses deceased-donor pancreatic islets, not laboratory-grown stem cells, and has a narrowly defined type 1 indication.
- Insulin independence in selected research participants is not evidence of a permanent universal cure. It does not establish removal of autoimmunity, lifelong durability, broad suitability, or applicability to every person with type 2 diabetes.

## Scene guardrails

1. Opening: keep the title as a question. No cure checkmark, disease crossed out, countdown to eradication, or universal promise.
2. Human context: portray an ordinary adult living normally. A glucose meter is one possible monitoring method. A CGM measures glucose in interstitial fluid, not direct blood continuously. Avoid showing everyone using identical insulin injections or sensors. Avoid clinical numeric readings unless properly contextualized; a neutral graph is preferable.
3. Research: label cell graphics «رسم توضيحي» and use «خلايا منتجة للإنسولين» or «خلايا بيتا» accurately. A useful secondary label is «من مسارات البحث، خاصة في السكري من النوع الأول». A stem-cell-to-specialized-cell sequence is a conceptual differentiation diagram, not instantaneous treatment or footage of an actual experiment. Do not depict new cells being injected directly into a pancreas unless explaining a verified technique.
4. Evidence: the proposed five modules are reasonable explanatory categories, not five clinical trial phases. Label them «مسار توضيحي لتقييم العلاجات». Safety and efficacy assessment overlaps clinical evaluation; long-term follow-up can extend beyond authorization. Do not attach completion ticks, success percentages, dates or an approval seal to all stages.
5. Knowledge: use questions rather than an unsupported table of established cures. «ما الذي أثبته العلم؟» and «ما الذي لا يزال قيد البحث؟» are suitable. If showing documents, make them clearly original editorial diagrams rather than fabricated journal articles or study results.
6. Book: retain the exact question title and visibly label the stand-in «غلاف مؤقت». Do not invent author credentials or published cover art. The narrative ending should communicate informed curiosity, not stopping ongoing treatment.

## Voiceover assessment

The supplied mission's wording about promising results, safety, efficacy, and the importance of evidence is appropriately cautious. Preserve the approved recording. A broad mention of «السكري» followed by stem-cell illustrations should be qualified in the research visuals to avoid suggesting these approaches already solve every type of diabetes. No claims of efficacy rates or availability are needed in the film.

## QA checklist for final imagery

- Research graphics are explicitly illustrative; no fake micrographs presented as experimental evidence
- Islet/beta-cell relationship is accurate and no universal diabetes mechanism is implied
- No fabricated outcomes, p-values, journal titles or author attribution
- Clinical progression is explanatory and safety assessment is not presented as a completed guarantee
- No treatment discontinued, insulin thrown away or universal cure symbolism
- Temporary cover is identified on screen
- Exact narration retained and no medical claim added by captions
